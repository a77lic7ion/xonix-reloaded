# Player, Biomes, and Settings Specification

The player marker becomes a **two-stroke X** rather than a diamond. Its intersection is calculated from the logical grid cell centre in every frame, so the centre remains exactly aligned with the safe rail or active trail. A small white core maintains visibility against every field palette.

| Stages | Field theme | Rail and claimed surface | Sea enemy | Rail enemy | Live trail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | **Luminous Abyss** | Mineral teal / deep blue | Electric cyan orb | Coral diamond | Amber vector |
| 3–4 | **Ember Reef** | Warm amber / charcoal red | Solar yellow orb | Hot magenta shard | Citrus-white vector |
| 5–6 | **Aurora Ice** | Icy cyan / blue-violet | Pale aqua orb | Orchid shard | Rose-pink vector |
| 7–8 | **Verdant Flux** | Spring green / deep teal | Lime orb | Fuchsia shard | Warm gold vector |
| 9+ | **Solar Rift** | Gold / midnight plum | Tangerine orb | Red-violet shard | Bright ivory vector |

The settings menu is a compact in-game overlay opened from the HUD. It pauses the simulation without obscuring its context, exposes music and SFX toggles as future-ready preferences, and offers a deliberate return to the title screen. No audio assets are added in this revision, so the toggles are clearly marked `READY` rather than implying sound is already active.
